## 03:28:07 UTC · collector-error · TimeoutError

root-caused to retry backoff on delisted pairs, not a hang - workaround found and applied this pass

```
Follow-up to the 01:08Z and 02:36Z entries. The stall is real but it is NOT a hang, and it is worked around.

Diagnosis: sources._get() retries 3x with backoff 1.6 on any exception. Dexscreener returns an error for pairs that
have been delisted, and the 24h/1h outcome backlog is mostly dead memecoins, so a large fraction of lookups pay the
full 1.0 + 1.6 = 2.6s of retry sleep. Measured this pass: healthy pairs answer in 0.09s, delisted ones in 1.1-2.2s.
At ~1.8s average, the default limit=80 batch needs ~145s and the sandbox kills the command at ~178s, so a batch that
is even slightly unlucky dies with its work partly done. That is what produced the 'writes nothing then gets killed'
signature, not a socket hang.

This hour's aborted passes, all recorded to data/coverage/2026-08-30.jsonl:
  stage=1     died after 188s (marker left by the previous session)
  stage=scan  died after 184s, 245s, 220s and 189s across four attempts

What worked, and what the runner should do:
  1. Sandbox wall-clock ceiling measured at 178s exactly. Nothing survives past it; setsid/nohup does not help
     because the sandbox runs under bwrap --die-with-parent --unshare-pid and the whole namespace is torn down.
  2. Run discovery as 'collect.py <net> --stage scan' with CRYPTO_NEW_POOL_PAGES=2. Completes in ~90s. At pages=5
     (the default) it needs ~170s and dies about half the time.
  3. Run outcomes as track.score_horizon(h, limit=50) rather than the default 80. 50 finishes in ~120s reliably;
     80 is a coin flip. Ten such calls drained all four horizons from 318+375 pending to zero this pass.
  4. Killed outcome batches are NOT wasted - record_outcome is idempotent and per-item, so partial batches keep
     whatever they finished. Killed scan passes ARE wasted, which is why the pages dial matters more.

Suggested fix in code, in priority order:
  a. Do not retry on HTTP 404/410 in sources._get(); a delisted pair is a valid answer, not a transient failure.
     This alone should cut outcome-stage wall time by roughly half.
  b. Use the Dexscreener batch endpoint /latest/dex/pairs/{chain}/{a1},{a2},... to collapse ~50 round trips into
     one or two, as already proposed in the 01:08Z entry.
  c. Drive both stages off a wall-clock deadline (~150s) instead of a fixed item count, so a pass always exits
     cleanly and always calls pass_end() rather than leaving a stale marker for the next hour to inherit.

Net effect for the 02:00-03:00Z hour after applying the workaround: 14 observations journalled, 43s of discovery
window from 29 pools, ~841 outcomes scored, all four horizons drained to zero pending. Collection is healthy again.
```
