## 1. ACCURATE? The all-pairs verdict delta

Rule fixed first in `PRECOMMIT_allpairs_recheck.md`, before any contract was
re-queried. Window 11,591 outcome rows on **4,874 contracts**, 24h to
2026-09-23 11:37Z. 4,874 paced calls is over 80 minutes, so two stated arms,
297 unique calls, 0 lookup failures:

- **ARM A**, every contract with a recorded multiple >= 2x, n=178 (a census).
- **ARM B**, seeded random sample of contracts recorded `gone`, n=120 of a
  1,516 population, seed 20260923.

⛔ **CORRECTION TO MY OWN EXPECTATION. The delta is small.**

| arm | verdicts changed |
|---|---|
| ARM B (`gone`) | **2 / 120 = 1.7% [0.5, 5.9]**; only 1 reaches TRADING |
| ARM A (>= 2x) | **3 / 178 = 1.7%** |

118 of 120 `gone` contracts return **NO_PAIRS** on the all-pairs token endpoint.
So `gone` was mostly right, and the single-pool bug corrupted our **description**
of tokens, not our verdicts. That reproduces the 2026-09-22 result exactly: 12 of
15 SHAPE labels changed, **0 exit verdicts changed**.

⚠️ **The limitation that matters, and it caps this number.** Both methods read
the same indexer, and Dexscreener preferentially drops a token once it has no
liquidity (`docs/GORILLA_ARCHIVE.md` §3). `NO_PAIRS` is evidence the indexer has
nothing, **not** evidence the pool is closed on chain. This measures whether
summing changes the answer. It does not establish the answer is true.

## ⭐⭐ The ten-minute check nobody does: BOTH LEGS of the pair

Frank's question: on the xStocks and PreStocks assets used as pair quote assets,
does the issuer keep **freeze authority** on chain?

**Answer: yes, on every single one, and it is worse than freeze.** 14 mints read
from chain via `getAccountInfo jsonParsed`, addresses taken from Jupiter's own
token search by tag, not from memory. **14 of 14 readable, 14 of 14 LIVE.**

| control | count | what it lets the issuer do |
|---|---:|---|
| `freezeAuthority` LIVE | **14 / 14** | freeze any holder's account |
| `permanentDelegate` | **14 / 14** | ⛔ **move your tokens with no signature from you** |
| `pausableConfig` | **14 / 14** (`paused: false`) | halt the entire mint |
| `defaultAccountState` | 14 / 14, value **`initialized`** | ⚠️ NOT a whitelist today, but the extension is present so the state can be set |
| `transferFeeConfig` | 6 / 14 (PreStocks) | **100 bps** on every transfer |
| `transferHook` | listed, `programId` **none** | no hook program set today |

Control is concentrated, not diffuse: **one** freeze authority for all seven
xStocks (`JDq14BWvqCRFNu1krb12bcRpbGtJZ1FLEakMw6FdxJNs`) with a separate
permanent delegate (`5aMNNLQJwAEeoemTEMkv5NVjqKwvvefRYCQ5Z67HFvEq`), and for
PreStocks **one address is both**
(`WV9PJN7XTmTLVwbutCLFxp8TyePee6Xq5mRq6Fti5Wc`). COPX also has both in one
(`2cVYpagTt7ZGc3mmTXBa7fAznUtx5DUu6aCq8uVDaf4a`).

⭐ **And these really are the pair leg, so the check is not hypothetical.**
All-pairs on the mint itself: **OPENAI is the quote asset on 14 of its 30 pairs,
COPX on 17 of its 30** (both `pair_count == 30`, so both are FLOORS). One OPENAI
pair is quoted in **MSFTx** and one in **ANTHROPIC**, so RWA-on-RWA pairs exist.

**So the conclusion Frank predicted holds.** A memecoin can have both its own
authorities revoked and still sit on a quote asset whose issuer can freeze,
seize or pause the thing you would be paid in. ⛔ **Our detector reads one leg.**
Running `safety.py` on the quote mint of every pair is a genuinely novel check
and it is cheap: one `getAccountInfo` per distinct quote mint, and there are few
of them.

⚠️ **Not established:** whether these authorities have ever been *used*. Presence
is capability, not an event. That needs signature history on each authority and
was not done today.
