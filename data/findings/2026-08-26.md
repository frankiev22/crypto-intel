## 10:09:35 UTC · collector-error · DuplicatePoolJournalling

collect.py is journalling the same pool twice in a single solana pass

```
Not a crash: collect.py solana exited 0. The 10:05 UTC pass reported '40 journalled' but contained only 23 unique pair addresses. 17 rows (42.5% of the pass) were duplicates, every duplicated pair appearing exactly twice.

Both scanner-hits this pass are affected: crocs and PISTASOL each landed in the journal twice.

Mechanism, traced through the code: sources.new_pools() loops page=1 then page=2 of GeckoTerminal /new_pools with time.sleep(2.2) between them and concatenates with 'out += d.get("data", [])' -- no dedup. That endpoint is offset-paginated by recency, so any pool created during the 2.2s gap shifts the window and page 2 re-serves rows that were already on page 1. scanner.scan() then iterates the concatenated list without deduping addresses, and journal.record() writes every row unconditionally. Confirmation that these are two independent enrichments rather than one row written twice: the duplicate pairs carry identical pair/token/symbol/score/liq but age_hours differing by ~7.6 seconds, i.e. two separate Dexscreener calls.

Prevalence: intermittent and longstanding, not a new break. Duplicate-row share by day -- 08-21 9.3%, 08-22 7.3%, 08-23 18.8%, 08-24 9.2%, 08-25 14.0%, 08-26 13.6%. Today 5 of 11 passes were affected (03:05 16.7%, 04:05 35.0%, 05:05 10.0%, 08:05 35.0%, 10:05 42.5%); the other 6 were clean. It tracks Solana launch rate, which is why it is bursty. This pass is the worst on record.

Blast radius, checked rather than assumed:
- Outcome scoring is NOT corrupted. journal.pending() collapses observations into a dict keyed by pair and keeps the earliest, and scored_pairs() dedups on (pair, horizon), so a duplicated observation cannot produce a duplicated outcome.
- Journal counts ARE inflated. 'observations 3069' overstates the work actually done; unique_pairs 2725 is the trustworthy figure.
- The weight derivation is the real exposure. Weights were trained on n=1099 observation-joined-outcome rows; duplicated observations enter as repeated training samples and over-weight whichever pools happened to fall inside a page-overlap window. I did not read the derivation code, so this is a risk to check, not a confirmed defect. Currently moot in production since weights_version is 0 (FALLBACK), but it matters before any derived weight set is activated.

Suggested fix: dedup by pair address in sources.new_pools() before returning, e.g. keep an ordered seen-set on attributes.address. Cheap secondary benefit -- each duplicate currently burns a redundant Dexscreener enrichment call, so the fix also buys back rate-limit headroom on exactly the busy passes where the scanner most needs it.
```
