## 02:13:50 UTC · collector-error · ValidationBudgetStarvedByOutage

cross-source price validation is silently skipped exactly when the dexscreener outage makes it most necessary

```
No traceback - this is a logic defect coupling two subsystems. track.py shares ONE budget (FALLBACK_BUDGET=8, line 34) between two different jobs: resolution fallback (line 83) and pricecheck validation (line 112). Both test 'fallbacks < FALLBACK_BUDGET' against the same counter. During a dexscreener outage the resolution fallbacks are spent first and exhaust the budget, so the validation gate at line 112 is never entered, and record_outcome() is called with price_verdict=None. The outcome is then written with price_confidence=null and realizable=true, because journal.realizable() only sees status and liquidity, not the absence of a verdict. The failure is inverted: the worse the primary source gets, the LESS validation the large multiples receive. This pass, 24h horizon: 8 of 8 lookups needed the fallback, and both multiples >=2x at that horizon went unvalidated (ETF 2.15x, ZODL 4.67x) while all six multiples at the 1h and 6h horizons were validated and four of them were quarantined. ZODL was recorded realizable=true at 4.67x with a reported liquidity of $1,263,969 that sits in the same cluster as three validated-and-quarantined observations from the same pass ($1,287,165 vs $40,959 on geckoterminal, a 31x source divergence). This is the FLORK failure mode the pricecheck module docstring was written to prevent, arriving through an unguarded side door. Suggested fix: give validation its own budget separate from resolution, or fail closed - when a multiple is at or above VALIDATE_ABOVE and no verdict could be obtained, record confidence as quarantined_unresolved and realizable=false rather than null and true. Fail-closed is the smaller change and matches the module's stated principle that the honest record is 'no price' rather than a number with a caveat.
```
