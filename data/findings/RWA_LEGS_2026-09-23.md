# The RWA quote legs: the research Frank asked for before we publish either way

**2026-09-23.** Frank: *"rwa stonk pairs seem to be safe. I understand they have
alarming readings but I think you need to research more into those. There may be a
reason nobody checks the other leg. Research the actual issuer terms before we
publish anything either way."*

---

## 1. He was right, and here is the reason

⭐ **A freeze authority and a `permanentDelegate` are what a regulated tokenised
security is REQUIRED to carry.** They are how an issuer complies with a court
order, a sanctions listing or a securities rule. **A tokenised equity WITHOUT them
would be the anomaly.** Publishing that as a DANGER verdict was wrong and would
have cost us credibility with anyone who knows this space.

⛔ **So `pair_legs` now publishes a DISCLOSURE, never a verdict.** The field is
`issuer_powers_disclosed`, the line is `disclosure`, and the wording says the
powers are expected.

---

## 2. ⛔ And two of our own sentences were FALSE about the chain

This is the part that matters more than the framing, because it was us crying
wolf about something that is not happening.

Read from the mint accounts, 2026-09-23:

```
SPYx  transferHook        {"authority": "5aMNNLQJ...", "programId": null}
COPX  transferHook        {"authority": "2cVYpagT...", "programId": null}
both  defaultAccountState {"accountState": "initialized"}
```

⛔ **We were saying "every transfer runs issuer code that can reject it" about two
mints whose transfer-hook slot is EMPTY.** No issuer code runs on any transfer of
either asset today. What is true is that the authority can install one later.

⚠️ **That is the `authority_live=None` error pointed the other way: not-happening
rendered as happening**, and it is the more damaging direction, because a false
alarm is the thing that makes a real one ignorable.

⭐ **Fixed by generating every sentence from the extension's STATE rather than its
presence.** `legs.power_sentence()` now says:

- `transferHook` with an explicit `programId: null` → *"a transfer hook CAN be
  installed, but the slot is empty, so no issuer code runs on a transfer today"*
- `transferHook` with the key MISSING → still the capability sentence, because
  not-read is not the same answer as empty
- `defaultAccountState: initialized` → *"new accounts can be forced to start
  frozen (it is `initialized` today, so they are not)"*
- `pausableConfig` with `paused: true` → **"⛔ THIS ASSET IS PAUSED RIGHT NOW"**
- `permanentDelegate` with the delegate readable → names the address
- `permanentDelegate` with the delegate UNREADABLE → still reports the power and
  says who holds it is unknown. ⛔ **The first version returned silence there,
  which is the same not-checked-reads-as-clean bug, and it was caught by the
  suite within a minute of being written.**
- `permanentDelegate` revoked to the zero address → nothing

⭐ **And `intel._leg_authorities` was a SECOND COPY of `legs.read_mint`** which had
already drifted (one generated sentences from state, the other from names). There
is now one reader and intel delegates to it. `exit_depth` having four copies is
already on the blockers list for exactly this reason.

## 3. ⭐⭐ The dividend mechanism is visible ON CHAIN, and it separates the two

| | SPYx | COPX |
|---|---|---|
| `scaledUiAmountConfig.multiplier` | **1.003909240011759** | **1** |
| `newMultiplier` | 1.005714560286254, effective 1781755200 | 1 |

⭐ **A rebasing instrument and a cash-dividend instrument are distinguishable from
the mint account alone**, without asking the issuer anything. SPYx's multiplier
grows, which is how an xStock pays a dividend without a transfer: the holder's
DISPLAYED balance rises while the raw token amount does not change. COPX's
multiplier is exactly 1, consistent with Backpack paying cash dividends and
passing corporate actions through.

## 5. ⛔⛔ The two legs are MATERIALLY DIFFERENT instruments

This is the finding, and it is the kind of checkable fact nobody publishes.

| | SPYx (Backed / xStocks) | COPX (Backpack Securities) |
|---|---|---|
| what it is | **a tracker certificate, NOT the share** | **a tokenised share held by a broker-dealer** |
| redeemable for the real share | ⛔ **no** | ⭐ **yes, 1:1** |
| move to a normal brokerage | ⛔ no | ⭐ yes, ACATS |
| voting rights | ⛔ no | ⚠️ not established |
| dividends | multiplier rebase, no cash | cash dividends + corporate actions |
| US persons | ⛔ **excluded by the issuer** | ⚠️ not established |

⛔ **Backed's own geoblock page says its products are prohibited for "US citizens,
US residents, or anyone who is otherwise taxable in the United States"**, and that
the securities are not registered under US law and may only be offered to
non-US transferees. `docs.xstocks.fi` says the same in its own words:
**"xStocks are not intended for distribution in the United States, to any US
person, or in any other prohibited jurisdiction."**

⚠️ **That is the issuer's statement about its own product. It is not legal advice
and it is not a statement about Frank**, and `pair_legs` says so in the warning it
emits.

⭐ **It also lines up with the SEC Innovation Exemption already on the board**
(`docs/MARKET_META_2026-09.md` §2), whose scope bound is that tokens must give
holders *"the same rights and privileges as does traditional NMS stock"*. By the
issuers' own descriptions, a redeemable broker-dealer share is closer to that
bound than a tracker certificate paying cash economics is. ⛔ **We are not
claiming either one qualifies** - that is a legal conclusion and not ours to draw.

## 6. What stays a genuine flag

⭐ **The transfer tax**, broken out as its own `taxed_legs` field, because it is
charged on the way in and again on the way out and is therefore a round-trip cost
rather than a yield: **VCF 800 bps, Circuit 400, and ZCAT / RAYCAT / PURR-sol /
LOOP / KNOTS 300 each.** ⛔ And KNOTS, LOOP and STONKCAT still carry a LIVE
`transferFeeConfigAuthority`, so the rate can be changed after you buy.

⭐ **Neither SPYx nor COPX has a transfer fee at all.** So the asset with the
alarming authority readings costs nothing to move, and the assets that actually
cost you money on every round trip have no such readings. **The alarming reading
and the real cost are on different legs.**

## 7. Provenance, separated, because it is not all ours

Every field in `legs.ISSUERS` is tagged:

- **`from_chain`** - we read it from the mint account: the names, the metadata
  URIs, the authorities, the extensions, the multipliers.
- **`from_issuer`** - the issuer's own file or site, with the URL: the terms
  links, Backed's geoblock and `docs.xstocks.fi` on US persons, and
  `learn.backpack.exchange` on 1:1 redemption.
- **`from_press`** - secondary reporting, labelled as such and never as ours: the
  tracker-certificate structure, the absence of voting rights, and that the
  issuer retains mint / burn / freeze / forced transfer as securities-law tools.

⛔ **Backed's own `legal-documentation` page could not be read: it geoblocks our
request** and redirects to `assets.backed.fi/geoblock`. So the primary terms
document is unread, and the instrument's structure rests on Kraken's distributor
FAQ plus press. That limit is recorded rather than glossed.

⛔ **Backpack's user agreement could not be read either** - it is distributed as
PDFs behind Google Drive links. Its US-person position and voting rights are
therefore **NOT ESTABLISHED**, and the registry says exactly that instead of
guessing.

⛔ **A quote leg with no entry in the registry has NO issuer research**, which
`pair_legs` publishes as `legs_not_researched` rather than leaving the absence to
look like nothing to report. On STONK that list is **SOL, USDC, VCF, tradebot,
USELESS, Circuit, RAY, TYTR**.
